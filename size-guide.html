UniFit Size Guide

Find Your Best Fit

Use the guide below to compare your measurements before selecting your uniform size.

Before You Choose

How to Measure

01

Chest / Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest or bust while keeping the measuring tape level.

02

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline. Keep the measuring tape comfortable and not too tight.

03

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips while standing naturally.

04

Length

Measure from the appropriate starting point down to the desired garment length.

!

Important Sizing Reminder

Measurements shown in this guide are sample sizing references for the UniFit project. Update these values using the school's official uniform measurements once available.

Need Help?

Between Two Sizes?

If your measurement falls between two available sizes, choosing the slightly larger size may provide a more comfortable fit. Actual fit may vary depending on the garment.
